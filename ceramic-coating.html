Ceramic Coating

What it is

Ceramic coating is long-term protection for paint that already looks its best.

A liquid coating is applied to the exterior and cured onto the paint, creating a durable outer layer that helps repel water, dirt and road grime while keeping the finish glossy for longer. It does not make the paint scratch-proof, but it does add a strong layer of protection against everyday wear.

Time in the bay

Ceramic coating is best applied after paint correction. The coating itself runs 5 to 6 hours.

Because the coating locks in the condition of the paint beneath it, correcting the surface first gives you the cleanest possible finish before it is sealed. The total time depends on the size of the vehicle and whether the coating is booked on its own or with correction.
