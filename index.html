Meet the owner

For Shah, the passion for cars came long before the business. He has always been drawn to the details, the finish, and the difference that careful, precise work can make.

That passion eventually became No Dust Allowed. What began with taking pride in his own vehicles developed into years of hands-on detailing experience and a reputation for meticulous work.

Every car is approached with the same level of care and precision. Shah sees each vehicle through from start to finish, with the kind of surgical attention to detail that comes from genuinely enjoying the craft.

NDA was built from that passion, and it remains at the centre of the work today.
