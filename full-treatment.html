The Full Treatment

What it is

Some cars need more than a clean. They need to be brought back to life.

The Full Treatment takes care of the entire vehicle in one booking, starting with a deep interior and exterior detail before moving into paint correction across every panel. Once the paint has been restored, a ceramic coating is applied to lock in the finish and protect the work underneath. Every stage builds on the one before it, giving your car the full reset from inside out.

Time in the bay

Eight to ten hours, dedicated entirely to your car. The exact time depends on its size and condition.

For the day, it has our full attention. That means every stage gets the time, care and precision it deserves from start to finish.
