Frequently Asked QuestionsFAQ

What car detailing services do you offer in Mississauga?

No Dust Allowed offers interior detailing, exterior detailing, paint correction, ceramic coating, headlight restoration and our Full Treatment package. Each service can be booked based on what your vehicle actually needs.

How long does a car detail take?

It depends on the service, the size of the vehicle and its condition. Interior detailing, exterior detailing and headlight restoration each run 1 to 2 hours. Paint correction runs 3 to 4 hours, and ceramic coating 5 to 6. The Full Treatment runs 8 to 10 hours on one car. We confirm the expected time when you book.

What is included in an interior car detail?

Our interior detailing service in Mississauga covers the cabin from top to bottom, including vacuuming, extraction where needed, carpets and mats, fabric or leather seats, vents, trim, glass, cup holders, seat rails and the smaller areas that are often overlooked.

What is included in an exterior car detail?

Our exterior detailing service in Mississauga includes a thorough wash, chemical decontamination, wheel and tire cleaning, tire dressing, glass cleaning and detailed attention across the exterior of the vehicle.

What is the difference between car detailing and a regular car wash?

A car wash mainly removes surface dirt. Car detailing goes much further, with deeper cleaning and more attention given to the paint, wheels, interior surfaces and smaller details throughout the vehicle.

What does paint correction remove?

Paint correction is designed to reduce or remove swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, oxidation and other imperfections in the clear coat. The goal is to restore clarity, depth and gloss to the paint.

Should I get paint correction before ceramic coating?

Yes. Paint correction is always completed before ceramic coating so the surface is properly prepared and the coating locks in the best possible finish.

How long does ceramic coating last?

The lifespan depends on the coating product used, the condition of the vehicle and how the car is cared for afterward. We can recommend the right option based on your vehicle and the level of protection you are looking for.

Does ceramic coating make my car scratch-proof?

No. Ceramic coating adds a durable layer of protection against everyday wear, dirt, water and road grime, but it does not make paint completely scratch-proof.

Can cloudy or yellow headlights be restored?

Yes. Headlight restoration removes the faded and oxidized layer from the lens, then polishes and seals the surface to restore clarity and improve the appearance of the front end.

Do you offer detailing packages?

Yes. Interior and exterior detailing are commonly booked together, and we can combine other services based on what your vehicle needs. If you are not sure which combination makes sense, give us a call and we can put together the right package for you.

Do I need to book an appointment for car detailing?

Yes. All detailing services are booked in advance so we can make sure your vehicle gets the time and attention it needs.

Where are you located?

No Dust Allowed is based in Mississauga, Ontario. The car comes to us, and there is no limit on how far you travel in, whether that is from Etobicoke, Oakville, Brampton or anywhere else in the GTA. The exact address is provided once your booking is confirmed.

How do I know which detailing service my car needs?

If you are not sure what to book, send us the details of your vehicle and what you want to improve. We can recommend the service that makes the most sense based on its condition.

Do you detail all types of vehicles?

Yes. We work on vehicles of different sizes and conditions. Pricing and service time may vary depending on the vehicle and the amount of work required.
