Exterior Detail

What it is

A proper exterior detail does more than make the car look clean.

We start with a thorough wash and chemical decontamination to remove the grime that regular washing leaves behind. Wheels and tires are cleaned and dressed, glass is cleaned, and every exterior surface is worked through with the same attention to detail. The result is a car that looks cleaner, smoother and properly refreshed.

Time in the bay

The time depends on the size and condition of your vehicle. Most exterior details run 1 to 2 hours.

We work through the exterior from top to bottom, giving the paint, wheels, tires, glass and smaller details the time and attention they need to leave the car looking properly refreshed.
